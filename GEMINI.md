# X-RAY extension

X-RAY (x-ray.me) is the map of who is doing what in Web3, read-only: actors (protocols, companies, funds, exchanges, chains, DAOs) with their numbers (TVL, market cap, fees, open roles, commits, funding), their links (investors, portfolio, tokens, chains, sectors) and every public move, plus narratives and the market regime.

- Resolve names with `search_entities` first (free, not counted), then pass the slug to other tools.
- A free account has a 25-call trial; after it responses are previews (`paywalled: true`, numbers hidden). Say so and link https://x-ray.me/pricing instead of guessing numbers.
- Keep the `source` link from responses in your answer so the user can verify.
- Recipes: see skills/x-ray/SKILL.md in this repository.
